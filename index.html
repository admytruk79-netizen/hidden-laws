About

A life of practice, study and transmission.

I'm Oleksandr Dmytruk — an author, teacher and practitioner based in Portland. My path ran through a rare Qigong lineage out of North Tibet, Reiki, ancestral and shamanic practice, and years spent listening for what sits just beneath ordinary experience — the astral and subtle-body layer most people only notice in passing. I was drawn to each of these in turn because every tradition I studied held a genuine piece of something larger, and none of them, on its own, held the whole. The longer I practiced, the more clearly those pieces pointed to one underlying architecture, which is what eventually became ASCEND — not a philosophy to take on faith, but a structure built to be tested against real, lived experience.
