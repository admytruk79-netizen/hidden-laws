Mission

To preserve, develop and pass on practical knowledge that helps people understand themselves, reconnect with deeper layers of life, and bring what they discover back into the world.

The aim is not to escape one world for another. It is to unite inner and outer, visible and subtle, ancestral and present, knowledge and experience — and make that connection useful in everyday life.
