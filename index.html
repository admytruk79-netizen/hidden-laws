Mission

To preserve, develop and pass on practical knowledge that helps people understand themselves, reconnect with deeper layers of life, and carry what they discover back into the world.

The aim is not to escape one world for another. It is to connect inner and outer, visible and subtle, ancestral and present, knowledge and experience — while keeping discernment, responsibility and ordinary life at the center.

About

A life of practice, study and transmission.

I am Oleksandr Dmytruk — an author, teacher and practitioner based in Portland. My work brings together Qigong, Reiki, ancestral and shamanic practice, energetic awareness, astral and subtle-body guidance, and the structured developmental system I call ASCEND.
