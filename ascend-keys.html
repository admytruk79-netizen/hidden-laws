One platform

Three practices.

All three live on Home, one tap away. When something asks more of you, it helps to see what is really being tested and what your next step is. ASCEND Keys shows you that from your own life, not from predictions.

Reflection

For a question.

Bring a question and choose a spread, from a single card to nine. Read each card in turn, then the synthesis brings them together into what is being tested and your next step.

  • One Card, Three, Five and Nine Card spreads
  • Seasonal Attunement for a change of season
  • The Shadow Integration Spiral, The Octave and The Wildcards with Premium
Relief

For when it is too much.

No reading needed. Pick what it is like right now, overwhelmed, tense, drained or stalled, and open a card made for that moment. Hold it for two seconds, then breathe with it as it slowly brightens and settles.

  • Sixteen Relief cards in four groups
  • No counting, the card itself sets the pace
  • "Relief now" from the app icon or a Quick Settings tile
The Journey

A year of transformation.

A year of change you can see, through all 108 Keys. One passage a day. Each Key takes three days, Encounter, Trial and Seal, and each day ends with one question about your life. Your answers go to your Journal.

  • Five books lead from the first Key to the last
  • A missed day waits for you, nothing resets
  • Your progress stays on your phone
Modes

Choose the practice that leads, or keep all three equal. The switch sits on Home, under the date.

The light can follow your room. While the app is open, the phone's light sensor moves the screen gently between day, dusk and night within your mode. The reading is used and discarded, never stored or sent.

For the end of the day

Wind down.

Eight images, from cool light to deep red, for 10, 20 or 30 minutes.

A soft glow on each image breathes with you, about six breaths a minute, slowing as the session goes on. The screen dims and ends in black by itself. Nothing flickers.

An optional tone, 432 Hz or a Solfeggio descent that follows the images, is made on the phone. Nothing is downloaded. From 7 pm Wind down appears on Home, and late at night the app offers it once.

Last updated November 16, 2026

Short version. ASCEND Keys does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The only third party involved is Google Play Billing, which handles subscription payments under Google's own privacy policy.

What the app collects

ASCEND Keys itself collects nothing. There is no sign-up, no user account, no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service built into the app.

What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps some information locally on your phone. Your reading history, Journal answers, saved cards and Journey progress. Your settings, such as language, light, mode and Wind down choices. Whether you have an active subscription, so the app knows which parts to open. None of this leaves your device and none of it is accessible to us.

Light sensor

If "Follow the room's light" is on, the app reads the phone's light sensor while it is open, to set the screen's light. The value is used at that moment and discarded. It is never stored or sent.

Payments

Optional subscriptions are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. Google, not the developer of this app, collects and processes your payment information. We never see, receive or store your payment details. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

Google Play Billing, for subscription purchases and renewals. No other third-party services are used by the app. If that changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what is sent and why, before the change takes effect.

Children's privacy

ASCEND Keys is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.

Data retention and deletion

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no server-side data to retain or delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device. To manage or cancel a subscription, use Google Play's subscription settings.
