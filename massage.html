Treatment Methods

The six forms of Energy Massage.

Point to a treatment or press it to learn more. These methods are selected and combined according to the assessment at the beginning of your session.

01 · FoundationBasic Harmonization

A complete energetic assessment followed by focused correction of the most significant imbalances. This treatment supports general maintenance, grounding, balanced circulation and overall energetic resilience.

02 · Seven CentersChakra Harmonization

Systematic work with the seven primary chakras, from Root to Crown. The treatment supports grounding, emotional flow, personal strength, integration, expression, clarity and balanced movement throughout the full chakra system.

03 · Three ChannelsNadi Massage

Work with Sushumna, the central integrating channel; Ida, the lunar and restorative current; and Pingala, the solar and activating current. The purpose is to balance receptive and active qualities while restoring central alignment.

04 · IntegrationKundalini Massage

An advanced stage used only after the chakras and primary channels have been balanced. It supports the harmonious movement of vital energy through the central channel without forcing stimulation, while maintaining stability and grounding.

05 · Twelve CorrespondencesZodiac Massage

A structured sequence based on the traditional relationship between the twelve zodiac signs and regions of the body. Treatment progresses from Aries at the head through Pisces at the feet to encourage whole-body energetic continuity and symmetry.

06 · Elemental BalanceFive-Element Massage

Balancing through the qualities of Earth, Water, Fire, Air and Ether: stability, flow, vitality, movement and integration. The goal is not to strengthen one element alone, but to restore harmony among all five.
